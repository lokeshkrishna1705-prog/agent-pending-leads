How the numbers work. Wrong Disposition = a ticket still pending whose disposition is anything other than Active or DNPU (e.g. Lost, Booked, Not Engaged – Disconnected, Service query) — it should have been closed. Not Worked Today = pending ticket with no update on the As-of date; Idle 2+ Days = no update for 2 or more days (tickets with a future follow-up date are excused). 2nd Attempt Missed = outbound ticket still on DNPU 1 more than 3 shift-hours after the 1st attempt (shift 8 am–9 pm). 🚩 Missed Pending Time = the agent set a pending date & time on the ticket and the ticket wasn't updated by that time. Avg Pending / Day needs the Pending_History tab filled by the Apps Script; until then it shows “—”.
